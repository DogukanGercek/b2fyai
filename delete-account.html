Delete Your Account

You can delete your B2fyAI account and all associated data directly from within the app. Follow the steps below.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the B2fyAI app on your device
  2. Go to your Profile
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Select Delete Account
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted
Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, including your profile, matches, and activity history, will be permanently removed.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, user type)
  • Your matches and connections
  • Your activity and event history
  • Your account credentials

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for up to 12 months after deletion as required by applicable law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention. Anonymized or aggregated data may be retained indefinitely as it no longer identifies you.

Need Help?

If you are unable to delete your account from within the app or have any questions, please contact us at b2bmatchofd@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.
